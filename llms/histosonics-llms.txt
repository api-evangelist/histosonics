# HistoSonics

> HistoSonics is a medical device company commercializing histotripsy — a non-invasive, non-thermal,
> non-ionizing sonic beam therapy that uses focused ultrasound to form a controlled bubble cloud
> which mechanically destroys and liquefies targeted tissue at a sub-cellular level. Its robotically
> assisted Edison Histotripsy System received U.S. FDA De Novo marketing authorization in October
> 2023 for the non-invasive destruction of liver tumors — the first histotripsy platform authorized
> anywhere in the world — and has since added CE Mark authorization in Europe. The science
> originated with researchers at the University of Michigan.
